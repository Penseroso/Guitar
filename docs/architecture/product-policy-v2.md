## 3. Physical product heuristic: exact definition

The policy is computed separately from Physical assessment and ranking. Its record contains version, geometry assumptions, variables, tier and every applicable reason. It never writes a Physical verdict or a classic-v1 term.

### 3.1 Coordinates and quantization

Use six physical string indices 0–5, high E side first, regardless of tuning order. Only fret states >0 create stopped targets; open strings and mutes are not targets. Use the full closed cell [wire(f−1), wire(f)] without an invented near-wire or fingertip offset.

`demand-geometry-um-v1` uses the request's validated Physical scale length `L` in integer micrometres (existing default 647700, supported 1..2000000), and fixed reference outer-string spacings **N=35 mm at nut, B=52.3875 mm at bridge**. These are the frozen constructed-electric assumptions, not the user's measured spacing. No nut-width substitution, radius, action, force, pad width or anatomy correction is made. Other tunings retain physical string indices; fret support remains 0..36. A particular requested hand/operation is handled by §3.3, not by changing these spacings.

Use the existing pinned integer table `q[f]=roundHalfUp(10^12 × 2^(−f/12))`, never runtime floating exponentiation for policy decisions. For nonnegative rational z, `R(z)=floor(z+1/2)`:

1. `Q=10^12`; `X[f]=R(L × (Q−q[f])/Q)`, f=0..36, in µm.
2. For stopped fret extrema lo,hi: **J = max(0, X[hi−1]−X[lo])**. If fewer than two stopped targets, J=0. J is the full-cell longitudinal gap.
3. Use half-micrometre spacing constants `N2=70000`, `B2=104775`. A string-center endpoint at wire f has numerator `Y(s,f)=(2s−5) × (N2×L+(B2−N2)×X[f])` and shared denominator `20×L`, yielding µm. Keep signed numerators exact.
4. A stopped target (s,f) has transverse numerator interval `[min(Y(s,f−1),Y(s,f)), max(...)]`. For each pair i,j, `gapNum=max(0,lo_i−hi_j,lo_j−hi_i)`. **T = R(max_pair(gapNum)/(20×L))**, or 0 for fewer than two stopped targets. T is the maximum pairwise transverse interval separation.

All demand-geometry arithmetic must use **BigInt or an equivalently exact integer/rational representation**. Convert validated integer inputs/table entries to that representation before arithmetic. No Number arithmetic is permitted for L×Q, L×(Q−q[f]), spacing numerators, signed Y endpoints, transverse denominators or their intermediate products, even when a particular input happens to fit safely. Keep rounded X values and subsequent J/T calculations in the exact representation. For nonnegative integer numerator n and positive denominator d, implement R(n/d) as exact integer division `floor((2 × n + d)/(2 × d))`, not floating division followed by Math.round. Convert final rounded J/T to Number only after proving they are nonnegative safe integers. Loop indices and already validated source integers may be Numbers before conversion; they may not cause intermediate multiplication in Number arithmetic.

Rounding occurs only where specified. J and T are nonnegative integer µm. Pairwise interval minima need not be simultaneously attainable. These are descriptive planar cell quantities, not a hand configuration. Existing Physical **W=stoppedWireSpanUm** is also retained, with its original numeric version; W is shown for provenance but receives no additional product cutoff, avoiding a second threshold on closely related longitudinal geometry.

### 3.2 Contact topology and simultaneous complexity

**Nstop** is the number of stopped strings. **G** is the unchanged `partial-cover-v1` minimum contact-group hypothesis count. **K** is the largest number of same-fret stopped targets in a compatible flat interval: choose a positive fret f and two targets a≤b at f; the interval is compatible iff no k∈[a,b] has `0≤state[k]<f`. Count targets equal to f within that interval. Higher frets and unplayed strings do not block this hypothesis. A single target is allowed. K=0 if Nstop=0.

Define **G1=0** when Nstop=0; otherwise **G1=Nstop−K+1**. G1 is the minimum number of hypothetical contacts when at most one compatible multi-target interval is used and all remaining targets are singles. This is a product-selected simplification of the frozen contact vocabulary. It avoids assuming several simultaneous barres merely because an unconstrained group minimum is small. K≥2 reports a possible multi-contact interval, not an established barre or assigned digit. Neither G nor G1 is called an actual finger count. Open/lower-fret barriers remain active. No monotonicity, raw-fret-width, root, register, density or corpus gate is added.

### 3.3 Unsupported operations and tiers

`Uop=true` if any of these applies:

- requested profile scope is not `generic-static-fretting`, or a `handProfileRef` requests unavailable individual analysis;
- omitted strings require left-hand damping and at least one string is unplayed;
- the existing thumb result says `reliedOn=true`;
- assessment reasons contain `unsupported-damping`, `unsupported-profile`, `unsupported-operation`, `unresolved-screen` or `conflicting-evidence`.

Allowing a thumb without reliance is not Uop. Requiring damping with no unplayed strings is not Uop. Mere span/group warnings and human-validation absence are not Uop. Malformed inputs, invalid metrics, programming exceptions or failed calculations remain typed errors; do not disguise them as a tier.

Evaluate in this order, with **inclusive ≤ boundaries**:

| Tier | Exact condition |
|---|---|
| `unsupported` | Uop |
| `compact` | not Uop AND J≤55000 AND T≤42000 AND G≤4 AND G1≤4 |
| `extended` | not Uop AND not compact AND J≤110000 AND T≤50000 AND G≤5 |
| `wide-or-complex` | not Uop AND neither compact nor extended |

All tier thresholds (55/42/110/50 mm, 4/5 groups, one multi-target interval for G1), priority of unsupported, and reference spacing selection are **product parameters**. None is an anthropometric bound. Coordinate equations and compatibility predicates are evidence-derived descriptive constructions; their assumptions and new integer quantization are explicit. The tier function has no PASS/UNCERTAIN input branch. U(c) is exactly the compact predicate. The tier does not decide Recommended membership; Surface v2 independently composes it with Physical status and vocabulary membership. Status and reason changes that do not change Uop or these variables cannot change the tier. This is an independent L2 descriptor, never a score penalty or REJECT certificate.

UI and details must explicitly distinguish **Recommended = membership in the versioned product recommendation policy** from **PASS / UNCERTAIN = separate Physical evidence status**. `Recommended + UNCERTAIN` and `PASS + outside Recommended` are intentional combinations. Neither status overrides the other. Preserve and display Physical warning reasons on Recommended candidates. Never describe an outside-Recommended PASS candidate as physically unsuitable.


## 4. Independent Layer 3

### 4.1 Exact practical-vocabulary-v1 contract

Use exactly the abstract templates/open forms in [proposed-vocabulary.json](../research/web-voicing-corpus-v2/sitewide-practical-audit/proposed-vocabulary.json), under practical-exact-translation-v1. Output is V:boolean plus internal matching template/open identity and placement. It is computed from the structural request interpretation/instrument and six states, with no Physical status, thresholds, U, score or UI input.

Scope is exactly six single strings, tuning [64,59,55,50,45,40], capo 0 (the current engine has no separate capo operation), fret domain 0..15 on all strings, standalone, identity realization, no explicit slash, audited 20 qualities and roots 0..11. Outside scope V=false and ordinary L2 fallback applies. Explicit view restrictions are applied later by M. Additional structural constraints never admit otherwise invalid allocations.

Closed offsets are high-to-low, -1 unplayed, minimum stopped offset zero. Placement p=min(stopped states); states[s]=offset[s]+p for stopped strings, with mute pattern exact. rootPC=(rootAnchor+p) mod12. B permits every integer p from 1 through 15-max(offset), inclusive. A permits only its enumerated (root,p) pairs: major-7 [0,0,1,1,-1,-1], (0,8)/(1,9); power-5 [-1,3,0,-1,-1,-1], (11,4)/(6,11). C permits only major [1,1,2,3,-1,-1] at root5, p1. Exact IDs/anchors remain those in the frozen manifest. Never merge harmonic reinterpretations or generalize across quality. Open forms require exact quality/root/six-state match and are never translated.

The expected domain is 1,530 unique matches: 1,453 unique observed allocations (1,454 source observations include one duplicate) plus 77 product-extrapolated placements. Source-observed and product-extrapolated provenance are distinct in internal validation sidecars. Runtime need not ship a root-by-root observation database or assert source provenance; a vocabulary match alone makes no source-observed claim.

Production ships only quality, mute/state pattern, relative stopped offsets, root anchor, bounds, A/C exact restrictions, exact open allocations and version metadata. No website SVG, prose, audio, finger annotations, branding, research provenance or mirrored root-by-root database. No network dependency. Stop only an operation requiring material beyond this abstract boundary and report it.

## 5. Surface v2 — executable composition

M(c) = current Physical survivor satisfying all explicit ordinary user filters.
V(c) = independent practical-vocabulary-v1 match.
U(c) = independent L2 compact predicate.
P(c) = Physical PASS.
O(c) = L2 unsupported-operation flag Uop.

`recommendedRepresentative = M && V && !O && (P || U)`

`recommendedFallback = M && !V && U`

`allOnly = M && !recommendedRepresentative && !recommendedFallback`

Recommended order is exactly classic-v1(all representatives), followed by classic-v1(all fallbacks). Preserve the original integer numerator, denominator110000 and numeric six-state tie-breaking inside each partition. Partition is surface order, never a score bonus. No frequency/empirical coefficient, one-shape anchor, position/family quota or vocabulary-absence penalty. If representatives are absent use all fallbacks; if both absent return exact empty. Never backfill from All or manufacture six cards.

All=M in exact Stage-9 classic or explicit near-position order. V has zero All eligibility/order effect. Direct lookup validates against the entire current survivor universe. A future certified REJECT cannot reach either surface. Unsupported operations cannot be bypassed by a vocabulary match.

The frozen 162 U-fail observations resolve as 141 V+PASS -> representatives unless Uop; 21 V+UNCERTAIN -> All only. V+UNCERTAIN+U may be Recommended; !V+U is fallback; !V+!U is All only. L2 thresholds are not retuned to reduce these conflicts.

surface-request-v2 is a separate {schema, surface:recommended|all, view:view-v1} wrapper, never a structural predicate. Recommended accepts classic order only; selecting near-position explicitly switches UI to All, while a contradictory API wrapper is invalid-view. Existing bare view-v1 session calls mean explicit All for Stage-9 compatibility; the live UI sends an explicit Recommended wrapper by default.

surface-cursor-v2 identifies request/profile, ordinary view, surface and all demand/vocabulary/translation/composition/order versions, including the last surface partition and original rank key. Reject mismatched keys and restart explicitly. No reference anchor or reference-manifest ordering identity. Ordering caches are request-local, bounded and version-specific. No state from another surface may splice into pagination.

First six cards, subsequent twelve; API sizes1..128, <=48 rendered cards, 32MiB retained-row accounting and 64MiB engine budget. Exact replay after eviction, no prefix cache, approximate completion, candidate cap or legacy fallback. Counts distinguish survivors, explicit-view matches and surface matches; incomplete counts are lower bounds. Clearing ordinary filters preserves chosen surface and context. Root/quality changes reset Recommended/default filters; context changes preserve surface/filter and revalidate selection. Selection survives surface/filter/page/order changes, even outside the chosen surface, with an explicit notice and no autoplay. Direct lookup and exact-MIDI playback remain independent of surface membership.
