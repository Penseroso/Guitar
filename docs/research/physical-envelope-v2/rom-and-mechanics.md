# Joint range and mechanical evidence

These sources distinguish measurement constructs; none supplies a population-calibrated simultaneous guitar-demand envelope. A joint angle is defined by landmarks, coordinate convention, posture and method. Passive, active, task-observed and functional ranges cannot be merged into a single numerical box.

| Source | Population/sample | Measurement and quantitative observation | Applicability and limitation |
|---|---|---|---|
| RM01: Ngeo, Tamei & Shibata (2014), *Continuous and simultaneous estimation of finger kinematics using inputs from an EMG-to-muscle activation model*. [Primary publication](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/1743-0003-11-122), [primary paper table copy, p7](https://citeseerx.ist.psu.edu/document?doi=362f9e4bd3ddd3c6ad74eb49e897e240b6d90eae&repid=rep1&type=pdf) | 10 healthy volunteers, 7 male/3 female, age 27±4 years; no sex-specific distributions | Active free-space flexion/extension, individual and simultaneous finger movements. Mean observed index endpoints (degrees, SD): MCP −39.97±15.00 /62.29±14.27; PIP −14.95±12.42 /72.55±16.87; DIP −16.96±13.97 /45.51±22.25. | Full fist closure was excluded for marker visibility. These are task-observed endpoints, not universal limits. The separate theoretical-range column is not a measured population. No joint covariance or fretting loads were imported. |
| RM02: van den Noort et al. (2016), *Measuring 3D Hand and Finger Kinematics*. [Primary PLOS paper](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0164889) | 3 healthy participants, age29.3±3.1 years; left hand, no sex strata established in retrieved methods | Inertial/optical systems compared across tasks. Index MCP ab/adduction task ROM24.7°; between-method RMS difference3.3°. Main-angle RMS differences3.3–8.4°. | Technical validation, not a population ROM survey. A total excursion of24.7° is neither ±24.7° nor an all-digit limit. Marker visibility prevented middle-finger analysis. |
| RM03: Reissner et al. (2019), *Minimal detectable difference of the finger and wrist range of motion*. [Primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31182129/) | 20 healthy volunteers; repeat measurements on two days; demographic strata not verified from abstract | Active maximal ROM measured by goniometry and3D capture. Most capture MDD5–12° and SEM2–4°; goniometry MDD12–30° and SEM4–11°. Joint-specific exceptions apply. | Repeatability quantities, not anatomical margins or population SD. Do not subtract an MDD from a guitar joint limit or equate numerical solver tolerance with measurement accuracy. |
| RM04: White, Coppola, Skomurski & Mitchell-Rekrut (2018), *Influence of age and gender on normative passive range of motion values of the carpometacarpal joint of the thumb*. [Primary publication](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0894113017300595), DOI10.1016/j.jht.2017.02.011 | 96 healthy adult volunteers, actual ages21–92; four age groups24 each,12 male/12 female per group; Massachusetts community recruitment | Passive CMC ROM using standardized small-goniometer methods: flexion21.7±6.8°, extension19.5±5.7°, abduction51.1±5.5°. The oldest group had a reported4.5° lower abduction than ages35–49. | Passive excursion supplies neither voluntary thumb-over reach nor opposition capacity around a neck. Full subgroup covariance/percentiles not extracted. Primary searchable abstract/sections available; direct page fetch failed. |
| RM05: Holzbauer et al. (2021; online2020), *Radial and palmar active range of motion measurement: reliability of six methods in healthy adults*. [Primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33019845/) | 29 healthy adults, dominant hands, assessed by three raters; demographics not extracted from abstract | Six methods measure active thumb CMC motion. These values are method-specific means without imported SD/percentiles. Abduction is not the full coupled opposition movement. |
| RM06: Sung et al. (2013), *Development of the two-dimensional biomechanical hand model for a guitar player*. Abstract-only, limited-condition evidence cannot calibrate population demand, success or comfort. No force values or regression coefficients are adopted as v2 parameters. |

No mean/SD has been converted to a fitted normal tail. No source angle is inserted into the unavailable frozen solver. The numerical rows remain reference/measurement evidence with their own populations and methods. Their main design implication is to preserve separate fields for pad position, orientation, load, coupled motion and measurement error.

The frozen session had already established that independent joint bounds are an optimistic simplification and a fixed DIP/PIP coupling ratio is not justified. That conclusion is reused as session-verified continuity, not a fresh audit of the missing original biomechanics file.
