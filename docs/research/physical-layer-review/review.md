Review date: 2026-09-13. Production baseline: `a79007f0919b5b7f34657caa99f6d30a787c2a49` (verified current HEAD).

The present `>5 groups` and `>180 mm` exclusions lack the evidence required for production physical REJECT. **Intended contract**

The fixed upstream contract is **Generator → Physical feasibility → Empirical / ranking**. Physical receives an already coordinate-consistent, harmonically valid candidate. Its question is whether the requested simultaneous sounding/omission conditions have adequate physical support under a declared instrument, hand/technique scope and evidence standard. It neither revisits harmonic obligations nor uses generator route, pitch monotonicity, popularity or ranking preference as evidence of feasibility.

| Status | Meaning and sufficient basis | What it must not imply |
|---|---|---|
| PASS | Clears the declared physical screen with no identified warning or unsupported requested operation. Record `heuristic-screen` as the present evidence basis. A matching audited pose can separately add `conditional-witness` evidence. | A measured human can sound it; the inferred groups are actual fingers; comfort, clean tone, endurance or transitions are validated. |
| UNCERTAIN | A heuristic envelope is exceeded, a required operation is unsupported, material configuration information is missing, or the relevant analysis cannot resolve the request. Retain the candidate and reasons. | Rejection, estimated impossibility, or an implicit low ranking. |
| REJECT | A checkable necessary-condition exclusion establishes that **every realization permitted by the declared scope** fails. The model must conservatively cover that scope, its parameters must support the claim, and numerical/proof failure must abstain. | One failed barre assignment, one failed optimizer run, one synthetic hand's failure, or a cutoff inherited from engineering policy. |

PASS is deliberately a modest screening conclusion. If a consumer requires a demonstrated playable hand configuration, today's heuristic PASS does not satisfy that stronger request. Human validation remains **absent for both evaluator accuracy and model predictions**; notation or taught-chord provenance is a separate evidence field. Do not call all ordinary screen passes uncertain merely because no human study exists, and do not hide that limitation behind the word PASS.

Production should declare static simultaneous fretting, instrument dimensions/defaults, permitted techniques, and whether `x` means simply unplayed or requires damping by a specified hand. Unknown personalized geometry must not silently become an average-person claim. The default scale is a disclosed completion, not a measurement of the user's guitar. Invalid structural inputs are upstream validation errors, outside these physical verdicts.

**Current behavior and full constraint audit**

The actual chord evaluator has only two direct negative reasons: `too-many-fingers` and `exceeds-hand-span`. The caller drops those shapes before constructing a voicing. Exploration separately labels accepted shapes uncertain above four groups or 95 mm; everything else is `within-model`. Thus `playable: true` already includes warnings and cannot be mapped directly to PASS. [Evaluator](C:/Projects/Guitar/src/domain/chord/fretGeometry.ts:183), [caller](C:/Projects/Guitar/src/domain/chord/voicingSearch.ts:123), [assessment](C:/Projects/Guitar/src/domain/chord/exploration.ts:67).

Disposition below distinguishes **assignment pruning** from **whole-candidate rejection**. A necessary incompatibility of a particular contact arrangement does not prove that no arrangement can realize the target.

**Exact decision policy recommended for the next design**

1. Validate that requested physical semantics and profile are declared. An unsupported requested operation such as strict left-hand damping yields UNCERTAIN. Missing human validation is always recorded, but does not alone force every default screen result to UNCERTAIN.
2. Compute descriptive facts and the existing contact-cover estimate. A blocked flat-barre interval removes that assignment only; remaining assignments still matter.
3. Apply REJECT only when a sound certificate covers the complete permitted realization domain and the assumptions justify that domain. A failed or incomplete exclusion attempt supplies no REJECT evidence; continue through the screening policy. Unsupported requested operations or material contradictory evidence applicable to the declared scope require UNCERTAIN.
4. Otherwise retain the current warning envelope: >4 groups or >95 mm yields UNCERTAIN, including values beyond the former hard cutoffs. Reliance on the current thumb fallback also yields UNCERTAIN. Keep reasons cumulative; the current early return can conceal a simultaneous span warning.
5. Otherwise return PASS with evidence basis `heuristic-screen`, not `human-validated` or `feasible-pose`. Attach any matching conditional witness separately. An offline solver's search exhaustion does not automatically downgrade a screen PASS, and a witness does not automatically erase reach/technique warnings.

**No existing whole-voicing hard rule meets the proposed REJECT standard for the generic production hand scope.** Leaving that branch unused initially is preferable to inventing a new cutoff. A future restricted contact model may certify that its own mode set is empty, but that remains UNCERTAIN for a broader production request. Even agreement across all 16 sampled profiles is not proof across a continuous hand/technique domain.

**What the frozen explicit model adds—and what its results establish**

The implemented model adds four articulated fingers with different lengths and assignments sharing a palm pose; lateral string spread/taper and board depth; contacts within fret cells; pad approach; occupied hand/self/board volume; parked unused fingers; and clearance of declared static ringing paths. A successful solve serializes a reconstructable, collision-audited pose. These are meaningful configuration distinctions missing from the scalar/group screen. The explicit model remains insufficient in specific, consequential ways:

- **Coverage:** no constructive multi-contact barre, thumb or strict left-hand damping. The 25 reference unresolved results are 12 exhausted searches, 11 unsupported multi-contact cases and two strict-muting cases. Seven of ten conventional original shapes remain unresolved.
- **Search completeness:** finite palm poses, sampled contact fractions and joint/IK branches cannot establish nonexistence. Open Em/A expose a shared-fraction restriction: assumed pads collide at sampled same-fret targets, while allowed independent contact fractions remove that local obstruction. This is a search/model distinction, not evidence those chords are impossible. Smaller-pad/wider-nut profiles yield witnesses under different assumptions. [Diagnostic](C:/Projects/Guitar/docs/research/physical-feasibility/solver-diagnostics.md:5).
- **Sounding and damping:** S18/S19 share `x 5 x 5 5 x`; unplayed omissions have a witness, required left-hand damping is unresolved. The distinction improves input semantics but does not solve damping. Static line clearance does not validate clean vibrating strings or fretting force.
- **Anatomy and mechanics:** the reference is a constructed skeleton, not a measured person on a measured guitar. Assumed joint bases/ROM, pad/link/palm solids and profile scaling are not population-calibrated. Force, tendon equilibrium, loaded joint coupling, wrist/arm support, tissue compliance and endurance remain outside scope. Numerical tolerances do not supply biological accuracy. [Evidence limitations](C:/Projects/Guitar/docs/research/physical-feasibility/biomechanics.md).
- **Conservative obstacles versus valid rejection:** an infinite cylindrical board can exclude poses outside a real neck's edges. Such restrictive simplifications can explain search failures but cannot certify impossibility in a larger real-world domain.
- **Negative certificate scope:** S11's necessary reach inequality covers the declared fixed skeleton using contact-cell separation and an upper bound on contact reach. Its ~429.6-mm production wire span already fails the current gate. It supplies a conditional mathematical explanation, not a new production boundary or a universal human exclusion.

Keep `FEASIBLE_FOUND`, `INFEASIBLE_UNDER_MODEL` and `UNRESOLVED` as separate offline evidence states with their original assumptions. Do not turn the model into a required verifier, let unresolved prune ordinary voicings, or award an unqualified playability badge. Its runtime benchmark also does not isolate the present physical comparator: the old fast comparison combined the hand gate with P5. No exact replacement speed ratio is warranted. **Recommended production architecture**

Retain a small deterministic Physical service with three separable responsibilities: compute facts, collect evidence, and assign policy status. This is a refinement of the current core, not a replacement by the explicit solver.

- **Input:** structurally valid allocation; instrument identity/dimensions and defaults; allowed techniques; open/sounding/unplayed/required-damping semantics; optional declared hand profile. Physical identity/cache keys must include relevant profile, semantics and model version, not just tab or generator route.
- **Facts:** stopped wire span, contact topology, open paths, omitted strings, inferred cover count and available contact alternatives. Label estimates as estimates. Preserve original target constraints during alternative assignment analysis.
- **Evidence:** versioned heuristic warnings; optional audited conditional witness; optional scoped exclusion certificate; unresolved/unsupported reasons; assumptions and explicit human-validation status. Attach evidence rather than collapsing every outcome into `playable`.
- **Decision:** implement the policy above as PASS/UNCERTAIN/REJECT with cumulative reasons, certificate scope and evidence basis. Distinguish policy envelope, model infeasibility and human impossibility.
- **Boundary:** preserve UNCERTAIN candidates as a first-class physical result. Do not silently map them to false, discard them, or impose a ranking penalty. Downstream integration requires an explicit compatibility adapter because current search sets `playable: true`, exploration rechecks/throws on rejection, and ranking consumes that boolean. This report specifies the boundary only; it does not change those consumers or their ordering.

Do not alter shared `classifyFrettedGroups`/ranking metrics merely to align names; that would violate the session's ranking freeze. Any future production migration must make the Physical contract explicit while preserving ranking formulas. A side effect on candidate admission must be reported as such, rather than disguised as a wording-only change. [Current integration](C:/Projects/Guitar/src/domain/chord/voicingSearch.ts:191), [exploration recheck](C:/Projects/Guitar/src/domain/chord/exploration.ts:76), [ranking boolean dependency](C:/Projects/Guitar/src/domain/chord/deductiveRanking.ts:306).

**Remaining evidence gaps**

The missing decisive evidence is independent execution evidence with documented fingering, hand/instrument/setup, ringing/damping requirements and success criteria. Negative trials must retain the distinction between failure of an attempted fingering and impossibility across permitted alternatives. Current records support neither a false-reject rate nor a human accuracy comparison.

Further gaps are: sound bounds over permitted contact modes; actual simultaneous joint/palm reach; calibrated pad/barre pressure and clearance; thumb/neck geometry; open-string ringing and specified damping; appropriate variation in hands and instruments; robust contact intervals and numerical certificates; and separate evidence for static success, repeatability, comfort and transitions. Sensitivity sampling cannot replace those bounds or population data. These gaps are recorded here, not a launch of an Empirical/Ranking review.

**Repository-wide boundary note and verification**
