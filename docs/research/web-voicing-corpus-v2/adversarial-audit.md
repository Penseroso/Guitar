The metric unit changes with the withholding track. Comparing each arm against its paired classic control is valid; comparing absolute NDCG across tracks as though their relevance sets and context weights were identical is not. Reachability and rank quantiles are descriptive observation-level summaries; recall/NDCG use the stated source-balanced context averaging. Rank quantiles condition on reachable targets and must always appear alongside mapping coverage.

## Provenance and eligibility challenge

The acquired educational view contains 51 native rows: 39 from the Matney/Niemuth University of Kansas handbook and 12 from Wikimedia material, conservatively merged into one Wikimedia family. It is a selected acquisition view, not a census of teaching sources. The final `curated-mapping-v2` summary retains all 51 rows: **47 mapped and four intentionally ambiguous preprocessing exclusions across 39 queries**. Artifact verification accounts for 999,184 complete integrated survivors and matches all 39 query census controls. The initial 51 source-eligible flags do not make all rows eligible observations: `mapNormalizedRow` rejects unresolved labels before counting votes. That two-stage distinction must remain explicit.

The first mapping had three additional parser failures, not genuinely unsupported qualities. The source-explicit spelling `Maj7` was absent from the conservative suffix parser. The [notation correction record](notation-corrections.json) documents acceptance of exactly that major-seventh spelling before empirical evaluator execution, preserves every raw label and retains both mapper hashes plus the previous source snapshot. The three affected KU rows are `037`, `038` and `039`. This is an auditable notation repair, not rewriting source labels to match sounded pitches or adapting to ranking results. The reference corpus contains no affected `Maj7` spelling; its existing mapping retains the original source snapshot/hash and is unaffected. Four grouped/conflicting labels remain unresolved rather than being forced into convenient formulas.

The [Wikibooks jazz lesson](https://en.wikibooks.org/wiki/Guitar/Jazz) independently demonstrates deliberate selection of root-position and first-inversion exercises; eligibility is not inferred merely from a diagram's appearance. The [Commons file record](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:G_major_jazz_chord_for_guitar_(root).png) supplies its author, public-domain dedication, rendering history, and use in the lesson. Graphic authorship and pedagogical selection are separate evidence. Other wiki translations, printable pages and reuse of these graphics do not create additional votes. Six acquired diagrams plus six explicit prose allocations form the inspected Wikimedia sample; unavailable diagrams remain missing evidence rather than negative observations.

The open-source arm's 7,070 records remain reference/inventory diagnostics. Initial shape origins in chords-db, ChordPro and UCI are UNKNOWN. Named maintenance contributions, MIT/Artistic/CC licensing and default product display do not certify independent manual selection. UCI records do not contain frets or pitch octaves. chords-db forks and product consumers share one upstream; JGuitar-derived collections are algorithmic. The Siege collection mixes generated and imported rows despite its broad curated description and cannot supply independent votes. No unknown/generated open record enters the curated evaluator. ## Leakage and universe controls checked

* The mapper calls the authoritative integrated `EngineSession`, enumerates the complete stored survivor set, and uses production score numerators and tie ordering. It independently checks the first 18 against the session result. It does not call an old exploration generator.
* Source IDs, native records, ambiguous/unsupported rows, exact tuning/capo assumptions and full physical-string allocations survive the mapping artifact. Positive relative frets are normalized upstream; open/muted strings remain distinct. All 3,283 chords-db sounding MIDI lists match the source after conversion.
* Exact identity uses the full allocation in the fixed standard-tuning, no-capo domain. Closed physical families remove fret translation while retaining mute/string pattern. Open families stay exact because moving open strings changes the physical realization.
* Source folds remove the entire source family. Global physical-family folds remove all matching physical families. Joint folds apply both exclusions. Quality stress removes the whole chord ID. Unknown sources cannot contribute bins, exact counts or family counts.
* Each source contributes at most one vote per exact support key, family key or feature value. Repeated pages, alternate finger numbers and transpositions cannot multiply source votes. Training uses mapped eligible records only; structurally absent but valid-query targets would remain retrieval misses. This conditioning limits inference to the integrated research domain.
* The fixed feature residual uses thirteen interpretable categorical descriptions and no exact-shape identifier, query identifier or source identity. It is a marginal source-support diagnostic, not a learned human-preference probability. Correlations among root, bass, fullness, register and open-string features can still produce redundant boosts; the design does not establish their independent causal contribution.
* Rerank lists are constructed as permutations of complete candidate objects, preserving physical status and baseline scores. Exact order is asserted for the abstention arm. Displacement of candidates without exact training support is measured explicitly. Zero direct residual does not itself prevent displacement by other candidates' boosts.

## What this review cannot certify

Two acquired curated families cannot supply two independent training families in a leave-source or joint source-plus-family fold. Those protected arms must abstain exactly; exploratory one-source scores cannot earn generalized idiomaticity. A lack of high-position, rootless, rare-density or other observations is an unresolved protection gap, not a passed slice. Even a slice present in only one family is weak evidence about transfer. Supported-domain counts are not calibrated confidence bands.

Source balance does not remove acquisition bias, educational-purpose bias, conventional-quality concentration, style differences or incomplete lesson extraction. The handbook and wiki materials demonstrate selected teaching forms, not a representative distribution of performances or of preferences. Common educational traditions may remain correlated even where no direct copying is identified. No canonicality band or popularity scalar follows from two families.

Physical-v2 quantities are descriptive guitar/contact geometry under an explicit reference profile. Their partitions are convenient analysis bins, not human reach thresholds, comfort, difficulty or playability labels. PASS/UNCERTAIN are production metadata, not target quality. The legacy-region indicator is an intersection of documented historical gates inside the integrated query, not a recreated historical UI candidate pool.

This is exploratory development. There is no untouched confirmatory holdout, no human preference validation, and no warrant to change production ranking. The corrected implementation is suitable for a bounded reference-catalog and external-agreement study. Any stronger conclusion must separately establish source independence, coverage and protected incremental value; numerical retrieval gains alone cannot do so.

## Completed-result review, without retuning

After the fixed run completed, its `complete.json` matched the reviewed evaluator hash and reported 880 conserved ranked lists. The following findings are an interpretation check of that single run, not another model-selection cycle.

* Exploratory feature recall@6 gains are 2.34375 percentage points in leave-source and 2.85714 points in global-family and joint withholding; recall@18 does not improve. The gains repeat the same two KU observations: F major moves from rank 7 to 6 and F7 from rank 7 to 5. They are not three independent replications. Quality withholding improves one of those observations. No observed feature recall@6 slice regression was found in the reported slices, but that does not certify protection.
* All 47 mapped core observations are PASS and within the retrospective legacy gates. There are zero core high-position, rootless, UNCERTAIN or expanded-region examples. Report those protections as absent. Do not invent an observed high-position regression; the actual failure is lack of relevant validation coverage. The open references include broader regions but have UNKNOWN original selection and cannot repair the curated held-out evidence by changing labels.
* Support-gated leave-source and joint arms return exact classic order because only one independent source family remains available for fitting. Global-family withholding can retain both source identities; its gains therefore do not independently validate transfer to a new source.
* The family-support diagnostic loses 4.2857 percentage points of recall@6 under global-family and joint withholding. The held-out family's own support is zero while competing families may be boosted. This demonstrates displacement of unsupported targets, not proof that a missing family is undesirable.
* There are no exact interpreted shapes with repeated family support when declared query context is retained. Four interpreted physical shapes repeat across both families when context is removed. Those are valid narrowly scoped presence facts, but neither those four nor the larger UNKNOWN-origin reference overlaps establish calibrated canonicality bands or a generalized popularity measure.

The strongest defensible evidence-level conclusion from these results remains a curated reference catalog with provenance and explicit missing coverage. The modest two-target feature gains deserve preservation as development diagnostics; they do not earn a ranking signal.
